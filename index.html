Food service notes

Confirm the current meal plan, timings and menu with the relevant hostel or LPU residence office.

  • Standard plans offer different meal combinations.
  • Some plans depend on minimum student uptake.
  • Non-vegetarian food is through an independent vendor adjacent to campus.
  • Published services include packed breakfast and soft-food support.

Arriving from outside Phagwara

Approximate distances · check current routes
01

By train

Phagwara Junction is the closest listed railway station, approximately 9 km from campus. Jalandhar stations are also options.

Plan your visit ↗
02

By air

Amritsar Airport is approximately 110 km away; Chandigarh Airport is approximately 150 km away.

Visitor travel details ↗
03

By bus or taxi

Phagwara Bus Stand is approximately 9 km from LPU. Local taxi and auto services are available in the area.
